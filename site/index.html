Add to Home Screen

Put Rinny’s Games on your phone like an app: one tap to all the games.

iPhone or iPad (Safari)

  1. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow).
  2. Scroll and tap “Add to Home Screen”.
  3. Tap “Add”.

Android (Chrome)

  1. Tap the ⋮ menu at the top.
  2. Tap “Add to Home screen” or “Install app”.
  3. Tap “Add”.
