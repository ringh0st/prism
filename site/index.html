Rinny's Lumipals

Bend the light. Light every gem. Lumipals is a free puzzle game for all ages, played right in your browser or from your phone’s home screen.

Play now Free to play · No ads · Works on iPhone, Android and desktop

How to play

🔦

Aim the light

Each level starts with a beam of light and a board full of mirrors, crystals and gems.

🪞

Bend it

Tap mirrors and crystals to rotate them, steering the beam around walls and splitting it into colors.

💎

Light every gem

Light all the gems to win. Solve it in fewer moves to earn up to three stars.

What’s inside

Hundreds of levels

Worlds that start easy and slowly add new ideas: color mixing, beam splitters, moving parts.

Daily puzzle

A fresh puzzle every day. Keep your streak going.

For everyone

Easy to learn for kids, hard to master for adults. No violence, no ads, no random rewards. Optional diamonds only help when you’re stuck.

Accounts

You can play right away as a guest, and your progress is saved on your device.

If you want to keep your progress across devices, you can optionally sign in with Google. Lumipals then uses your name, email address and profile picture only to create your account and save your progress. Read the details in our Privacy Policy.
